My Anime Orgin Story

Every fan has a starting point, including the very person that has made this very website. I began watching anime around 2020 during the Anime rave, like anyone else during the 2020 covid time i was very bored and felt like i needed to seek a form of entertainment, luckily that has been anime for me. From the exciting adventures of Hunter X Hunter to the sad story of Voilet Evergarden it has truly inspired me to proceed with working on a website like this and i truly hope it resonates with many others!
